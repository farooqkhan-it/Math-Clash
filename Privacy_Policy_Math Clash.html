Privacy Policy

Math Clash
Last Updated: 17 September 2026

Welcome to Math Clash. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Math Clash handles information when you use our mobile application.

1. About Math Clash

Math Clash is a simple educational mathematics game designed to help users practice basic arithmetic skills in an enjoyable and interactive way.

The application provides mathematical questions based on operations such as:

  • Addition (+)
  • Subtraction (-)
  • Multiplication (×)
  • Percentage (%)

The application does not require users to create an account or provide personal information in order to play the game.

We do not use analytics or tracking services to collect information about how individual users use the application.

2. Game Data

Math Clash may temporarily process information necessary to provide the basic functionality of the game, such as calculating answers, displaying questions, keeping track of the current game session, and displaying scores.

This information is used only for the operation of the game and is not collected or transmitted to us as personal information.

3. Internet Connection

Math Clash does not require users to provide personal information through an internet connection.

If the application does not require an internet connection for its core gameplay, the game can be used without submitting personal information to an external server.

4. Third-Party Services

Math Clash does not use third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising services to collect users' personal information.

The application does not intentionally share user information with third parties because the application does not collect personal user information.

5. Children's Privacy

Math Clash is an educational mathematics application that may be suitable for children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Because Math Clash does not require users to create accounts or provide personal information, users can use the application without submitting personal details.

6. Data Security

Since Math Clash does not collect or maintain personal user information, there is no personal user database maintained by us for the application.

We nevertheless take reasonable steps to keep the application secure and functioning properly.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the functionality of Math Clash changes or if necessary to reflect changes in applicable requirements.

Any updated version of this Privacy Policy will be published on this page or through the application.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or Math Clash, you can contact us through the appropriate support channel associated with the application.

9. Privacy Policy Summary

In simple terms, Math Clash does not collect personal information from its users. The application is designed to provide mathematical questions and gameplay using basic arithmetic operations such as addition, subtraction, multiplication, and percentage calculations.

We do not sell, rent, or share users' personal information because we do not collect such information through the application.
